This release coincides with the one-year anniversary of the indie adventure puzzle game, which has captivated players with its stunning visuals and immersive storytelling.

Composed by Pav Gekko, the soundtrack draws inspiration from renowned Icelandic artists like lafur Arnalds and Sigur Rs, aiming to create a unique auditory landscape that reflects the game's enchanting environments. With a total of 93 tracks, including the new additions, fans can expect a rich tapestry of sound that enhances the emotional depth of their journey through the game's ancient world.
