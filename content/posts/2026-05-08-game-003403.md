The Daily Mail has just launched Relink, an innovative puzzle game that challenges players to engage in lateral thinking and uncover hidden connections. This daily brainteaser presents a grid of 16 words or phrases, where players must identify three related terms and one imposter in each row. The twist? The imposters are cleverly linked by a common theme, making the game not just about elimination but also about discovery.

With four lives to start, players must think critically and strategically to avoid mistakes. Each day at midnight, a new puzzle awaits, encouraging users to return and keep their streak alive. Whether you're a casual gamer or a puzzle aficionado, Relink promises to be a delightful addition to your daily routine.
