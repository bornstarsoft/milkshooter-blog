In a bold move that could reshape the gaming landscape, Arjan Brussee, co-founder of Guerilla Games and former technical director at Epic Games, is developing a new game engine called 'The Immense Engine.' This initiative aims to provide a European alternative to dominant American and Chinese engines like Unreal and Unity, focusing on compliance with European regulations and guidelines.

Brussee emphasizes the growing importance of creating usable 3D worlds beyond gaming, highlighting potential applications in sectors like defense and logistics. He envisions a game engine that leverages new construction principles and fully integrates artificial intelligence, allowing for more efficient development processes. According to Brussee, the rise of AI presents an opportunity to rethink how crucial software is developed, enabling a streamlined approach that could significantly reduce the manpower required.

As the gaming industry continues to evolve, Brussee's vision raises intriguing questions about the future of game development in Europe. Could 'The Immense Engine' pave the way for a new era of innovation and creativity in the gaming world? Only time will tell.
