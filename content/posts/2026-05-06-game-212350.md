In a recent discussion, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick shed light on Rockstar Games' strategy of releasing titles like Grand Theft Auto 6 on consoles before PC. This approach is rooted in the desire to prioritize the "core consumer," which Zelnick defines as the audience that is most passionate and engaged with the game at launch. Historically, Rockstar has favored console releases, and this decision reflects their commitment to serving this primary demographic first.

Zelnick noted that while the PC market has grown significantly, accounting for up to 50% of sales for major titles, the company believes that starting with console releases ensures they meet the expectations of their most dedicated fans. This strategy has been a hallmark of Rockstar's approach, as they often take years to release PC versions after the initial console launch.

As the gaming landscape continues to evolve, it will be interesting to see how Rockstar balances its core consumer strategy with the growing demand from PC gamers. Will we see a shift in their approach in the future, or will the console-first strategy remain steadfast?
